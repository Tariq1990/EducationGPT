1. SYSTEM OVERVIEW & WORKFLOW ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The KP Education Monitoring Authority (KPEMA), formerly the Independent Monitoring
Unit (IMU), operates a centralized digital monitoring framework across all 
settled and merged tribal districts of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Field Monitoring Officers 
(FMOs/Monitors) conduct routine monthly visits using GPS-enabled mobile apps 
to verify school functionality, teacher/staff presence, student enrollment, and 
physical basic infrastructure. 

Data flows into the central relational database, surfaced through the IMU Dashboard:
- Monthly monitoring cycles run on a calendar basis (e.g., Aug-2026, Sep-2026).
- Roster assignments define monthly inspection targets per monitor.
- Re-visit protocols verify corrective actions on absent teachers or closed schools.
- Annual School Census (ASC) data serves as the baseline for sanctioning posts, 
  enrolment tracking, and infrastructure allocation.

3. DASHBOARD FILTER CONTROLS & QUERY SELECTORS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The dashboard top control bar filters all downstream charts and report queries:
- Form Name: `dashboard_serch` -> Action: `dashboard.php` (POST)
- Form Fields:
  1. `dashboard_date`: Month-Year string (Format: `M-YYYY`, e.g., `Aug-2026`).
  2. `district`: District identifier slug (e.g., `mohmand`, `peshawar`, `swat`).
  3. `thesil`: Tehsil dropdown populated via AJAX (`populate_tehsiles(...)`).
     Key Mohmand Tehsils: SAFI, PANDIALI, AMBAR, KHWEZAI, PRANG GHAR, HALIMZAI, EKKA GHUND.
  4. `ucs`: Union Council dropdown populated via AJAX (`populate_ucs(...)`).
  5. `gender`: `Boys` | `Girls` | `` (Both).
  6. `school_level`: `Mosque` | `Primary` | `Middle` | `High` | `Higher Secondary` | `` (All).
  7. `isSettled`: Hidden field (`0` for Newly Merged Districts/NMD, `1` for Settled).


4. EMBEDDED DASHBOARD DATA MODELS (JAVASCRIPT STATE VARIABLES)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Upon loading `dashboard.php`, the backend injects real-time JSON state variables:

A. `surveydata`:
   - `totalsurveysdone`: Total schools surveyed in current month.
   - `schoolscount`: Total target schools registered in district.
   - `totalsurveypercentage`: Coverage percentage (`totalsurveysdone / schoolscount * 100`).
   - `totalboys`, `totalgirls`: Gender breakdown counts.
   - `rural`, `urban`: Locality counts and percentages.
   - `mosquecount`, `primarycount`, `middlecount`, `highcount`, `highseccount`: Level counts.

B. `schoolStatusData`:
   - `totalschools`: Total surveyed schools evaluated.
   - `openschools`, `closedschools`: Functioning vs locked/closed schools.
   - `openschoolspercentage`, `closedschoolspercentage`.
   - `studentstaffabsent`: Schools where both students and teachers are missing.
   - `nonfunctionalschool`: Non-operational facilities.

C. `TeacherPresenceData`:
   - `totalteachers`: Total sanctioned/observed teaching staff count.
   - `absent`: Total absent teachers.
   - `percentage`: Overall teacher absence rate.
   - `sanctionedleave`: Authorized leave count & percentage.
   - `unauthorizedabent`: Un-authorized absence (disciplinary action trigger).
   - `latecomer`: Late arrival count & percentage.
   - `onduty`: Teachers deployed on other official duty (exams, election, census).
   - `proxyteachers`: Discovered illegal substitute teachers.
   - `absentruralpercentage`, `absenturbanpercentage`, `absentmalepercentage`, `absentfemalepercentage`.

D. `non_teacherPresence`:
   - Non-teaching staff presence, absence, unauthorized absence rate, on-duty metrics.

E. `facilities`:
   Evaluates 5 Essential Basic Facilities (Available vs Functional):
   1. Electricity: `totalelectricityavailable`, `totalelectricityfunctional`
   2. Water: `totalwateravailable`, `totalwaterfunctional`
   3. Toilet: `totaltoiletavailable`, `totaltoiletfunctional`
   4. Boundary Wall: `boundarywallavailable`, `boundarywallfunctional`
   5. Building: `buildingavailable`
   6. Playground: `playgroundavailable`
   - `totalfacilitiespercentage`: Composite infrastructure functionality index.

F. `students_attendance`:
   - `totalstudents`: Total enrollment in surveyed schools.
   - `totalabsentstudents`: Absent student headcount.
   - `totalabsentstudentspercent`: Student absenteeism rate.
   - Gender breakdowns: `totalgirlsabsent`, `totalboysabsent` (% and count).
   - Level breakdowns: `middleabsent`, `highabsent`, `highsecondaryabsent`.
   - Locality breakdowns: `totalurbanabsent`, `totalruralabsent`.

G. `vacantTeachers` & `singleTeacherSchools`:
   - Vacant posts across Primary, Middle, High, and Higher Secondary levels.
   - `singleTeacherSchools`: Schools running with only 1 teacher (critical vulnerability).

H. `villegalBuildingStatus`:
   - `whole`: Wholly illegally occupied school buildings.
   - `part`: Partially illegally occupied school premises.
   - `teachersbyotherngo`: Staff appointed by external bodies.

I. `TopDistricts` / `TopTehsils`:
   - Comparative ranking of tehsils/districts across Surveys, Teachers, and Students attendance.


5. FULL REPORTING DIRECTORY & URL QUERY SCHEMAS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
All deep drill-down reports follow the standardized HTTP GET parameter schema:
Endpoint: `reports.php?r=<report_slug>&districtCommaSep=<district>&reports_start_date=<date_range>`
Example: `reports.php?r=basic-facilities&districtCommaSep=mohmand&reports_start_date=08-01-2026 - 08-30-2026`

REPORT CATEGORIES & SLUGS:

A. SURVEYS
- `monitor-wise-survey-done`: Surveys completed per individual monitoring officer.
- `monitor-wise-coverage`: Target vs visited coverage percentage by officer.
- `roaster-assignment`: Monthly inspection schedule assigned to each monitor.
- `school-not-surveyed`: List of schools missed during the active monitoring cycle.
- `monitor-daily-visits`: Daily log of geo-tagged school visits.
- `survey-done`: Aggregate list of surveyed schools with timestamps.
- `survey-timing-report`: Arrival and departure timestamps logged at each school.

B. SCHOOLS
- `closed-schools`: Detailed list of schools found closed during visits with reasons.
- `partially-occupied-schools`: Schools with unauthorized partial occupation.
- `wholly-occupied-schools`: Schools fully occupied by unauthorized entities.
- `building-availability`: Ownership and physical building existence verification.
- `basic-facilities`: Detailed matrix of water, electricity, toilet, and boundary wall status.
- `adminisrative-visits`: Visits logged by ADO, SDO, and District Education Officers (DEO).
- `schoolleaders-visits`: Academic and instructional mentoring visits by School Leaders.
- `ptc-council`: Parent Teacher Council functionality, bank account status, and meetings.
- `single-teacher`: Schools with single teacher deployment.

C. ENROLLMENT / BOOKS
- `ftb-new-application`: Free Text Books delivery, requirement, and shortfall tracking.
- `class-wise-enrollment`: Grade-by-grade enrollment roster (Nursery to Class 12).
- `enrollment-gap`: Retention and drop-out analysis between consecutive grades.
- `stipend`: Stipend distribution verification for female students.
- `new-enrollment`: New admissions logged in the ongoing academic year.

D. TEACHING STAFF
- `absent-teaching-staff-unauth`: Un-authorized absent teachers flagged for salary deduction.
- `teaching-staff-attendance`: Comprehensive attendance ledger for teachers.
- `vacant-posts`: Unfilled sanctioned teaching posts by designation.
- `teacher-wrong-posting`: Teachers posted against mismatched subjects or cadres.
- `teachers-appointed-by-otherorg`: Teachers hired by NGOs, PTC, or partner projects.
- `teacher-search.php`: Direct search tool by CNIC, Personal No, or Teacher Name.

E. NON TEACHING STAFF
- `absent-non-teaching-staff-unauth`: Un-authorized absence of lab assistants, peons, chowkidars.
- `non-teaching-staff-attendance`: Non-teaching attendance register.
- `vacant-nonteaching-staff`: Unfilled non-teaching sanctioned posts.
- `non-teacher-search.php`: Direct search for non-teaching staff by CNIC or Name.

F. REVISITS
- `teaching-staff-attendance-revisit`: Secondary visit verification of teacher attendance.
- `absent-teaching-staff-unauth-revisit`: Revisit confirmation of unauthorized absence.
- `non-teaching-staff-attendance-revisit`: Secondary verification of non-teaching staff.
- `absent-nonteaching-staff-unauth-revisit`: Revisit confirmation of non-teaching absence.
- `school-revisit`: General follow-up visit to closed or disputed schools.
- `student-enrollment-revisit`: Headcount verification to eliminate ghost students.

G. GCS SCHOOLS (Government Community Schools)
- `gcs-schools-information`: Profile of community-managed schools.
- `gcs-student-attendance`: Community school student attendance rates.
- `gcs-teacher-attendance`: Community school teacher attendance logs.

H. PROXY STAFF
- `proxy-staff`: Discovered proxy staff cases (persons teaching in place of appointed teachers).

I. ASC (Annual School Census) INTEGRATION
- `furniture`: Student desks, chairs, and teacher furniture requirements vs available.
- `infrastructure`: Classroom condition (Pakka/Kacha), land area, covered area.
- `sanctioned-filled-teaching-staff`: Complete census of sanctioned, filled, and vacant teaching posts.
- `sancioned-filled-non-teaching-staff`: Census of non-teaching posts.
- `classwise-enrollment`: Annual census class-wise student strength.
- `census-teacherInfo`: Qualifications (BPS, academic degree, professional certification).
- `census-nonteacherInfo`: Non-teacher demographic and grade details.
- `census-agewise-enrollment`: Age vs grade appropriateness and over-age student counts.
- `census-disabled-students`: Special needs and disability categorization.
- `census-ptc-info`: PTC committee formation, account balance, and annual fund receipt.

J. DOUBLE SHIFT SCHOOLS (DSS)
- `dss-teachers`: Teachers deployed in morning vs afternoon shifts.
- `dss-nonteachers`: Shift staff allocations.
- `dss-schoolvisits`: Shift monitoring visits.
- `dss-stdenrollment`: Afternoon shift student attendance and enrollment.

K. SPECIAL TASK & DISASTER MONITORING
- `schools-list.php`: Flood-affected and disaster-damaged schools.
- `specialTaskSurvey-done`: Specific infrastructure rehabilitation surveys.
- `specialTaskClassRooms`: Classroom reconstruction tracking.
- `specialTaskEnrollment`: Post-disaster student return rates.
- `specialTaskExistingFurniture` & `specialTaskNewFurniture`: Emergency furniture provision.
- `specialTaskPTCFund`: Emergency grant utilization from PTC accounts.

7. KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS (KPIs) & CALCULATION STANDARDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Student-to-Teacher Ratio (STR):
   STR = Total Enrolled Students / Total Present (or Filled) Teachers
   Standard Threshold: Ideal <= 40:1. Critical > 50:1.

2. Student-to-Classroom Ratio (SCR):
   SCR = Total Enrolled Students / Number of Pakka (Permanent) Classrooms
   Standard Threshold: Ideal <= 45:1. Critical > 60:1.

3. Teacher Unauthorized Absence Rate:
   Unauth Absent Rate = (Unauthorized Absent Teachers / Total Sanctioned Posts) * 100
   Red Flag: > 5% triggers disciplinary alert.

4. Basic Facilities Functionality Index (BFFI):
   BFFI = (Electricity_Func + Water_Func + Toilet_Func + BoundaryWall_Func + Building_Func) / 5
   Scored from 0.0 to 1.0 (or 0% to 100%).

5. Single Teacher School Risk:
   Any Primary or Middle school with single teacher serving multiple grades is classified 
   as HIGH VULNERABILITY regardless of enrollment size.

6. PTC Fund Utilization Efficiency:
   Utilization Rate = (Expenditure) / (Opening Balance + Government Grant Received)
   Idle Fund Risk: Present Balance > PKR 100,000 while basic facility deficits exist.
